    Line: expected uncertainty at your assumed noise. Dot: what this particular simulated sample gives. Dashed lines: your target and 12 months.

    Method: ASHRAE Guideline 14 fractional savings uncertainty, 1.26 × t × (CV(RMSE) ÷ F) × √((1 + 2/n) ÷ m), with a three-parameter (base, heating, cooling) monthly model at a 60°F balance point. It assumes independent monthly residuals. Interval data needs the autocorrelation adjustment.
