CV is variation divided by the average. Higher variation usually means more cycles are needed.

Quick planning estimate for an average across independent cycles: cycles ≈ (1.96 × CV ÷ desired precision)². Example: 20% variation and ±10% precision gives about 16 cycles. Refine for small samples, related cycles, missing data, and distinct operating groups. Use this only for cycle averages; it does not prove savings or validate a forecast.

Energy-use factor means something that changes energy use, such as outdoor temperature, production, or operating hours. The sliders use a made-up scale from 0 to 100; they do not represent a particular site.
